Case study — Training & Capacity Development

Vinsighte: Strengthening Sales & Customer Service

How Vantecrest designed and delivered a one-week workshop to strengthen how Vinsighte's staff engage customers.

The Challenge

Vinsighte wanted to strengthen the capabilities of its staff across customer service and sales, particularly in how they engage customers, communicate value, and support the customer journey.

What Vantecrest Did

Vantecrest designed and delivered a one-week workshop for Vinsighte's staff, covering both customer service and sales.

The sessions focused on practical skills that staff could apply directly in their day-to-day interactions with customers and prospects.

The Solution

A one-week staff development programme covering customer service and sales was delivered, giving the team practical frameworks and techniques for improving customer interactions and sales conversations.

The Outcome

Vinsighte's staff completed a focused one-week training programme designed to strengthen their customer service and sales capabilities and improve how they engage with customers.
