About Vantecrest

A problem-solving concierge for businesses.

Businesses rarely have just one problem. A growth challenge may be a sales problem. A sales problem may be a market problem. A market problem may come back to strategy, structure or execution.

Vantecrest exists to help businesses find the real problem and solve it. We combine business strategy, market research, commercial development, technology and capability building to help organisations make better decisions and execute them effectively.

Mission

To make high-quality business thinking and practical solutions accessible to organisations seeking to grow, adapt and create lasting value.

Vision

To shape a stronger generation of African businesses through better decisions, stronger capabilities and sustainable growth.

Integrity

We do what is right, even when it is difficult.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to a high standard in everything we do.

Insight

We seek to understand before recommending.

Collaboration

We work with clients, not just for them.

Impact

We measure our work by the value it creates.
